03 · THREE-LINK GUIDE03 · 三連桿教學

Three links: solve the wrist first.三連桿：先求手腕位置。

A gripper can point in different directions at the same location. If you specify both the tip position and its direction, the last link tells you exactly where the wrist must be.

夾爪在同一位置可以指向不同方向。若同時指定末端位置和方向，第三段連桿就能唯一決定手腕在哪裏。

01 · STEP BACK FROM THE TIP01 · 由末端往回走

Find the wrist W.找出手腕 W。

Let T = (x, y) be the red target and ψ the absolute direction of link 3, measured from +x. From T, step back by one link length in that direction to get the blue wrist W.

令 T = (x, y) 為紅色目標，ψ 為第三段相對正 x 軸的絕對方向。從 T 沿該方向往回走一段連桿的長度，就得到藍色手腕 W。

W = T − L₃(cos ψ, sin ψ)

= (1.65, 0.00) − 0.75(cos 30°, sin 30°)

W ≈ (1.00, −0.38)

In this diagram, the target slider moves T along the positive x-axis. The direction slider moves W around T while T stays fixed.

在這張圖中，目標滑桿讓 T 沿正 x 軸移動。調整方向滑桿時，T 留在原位，W 則繞着 T 移動。

02 · REUSE THE TWO-LINK SOLVER02 · 沿用雙連桿解法

Reach W, then turn the last joint.到達 W，再轉動最後一個關節。

Treat W as the target for links 1 and 2. Since both have length 1, their reachable region is the disc of radius 2. If W is outside that disc, no set of angles can reach the specified position and direction. In this demo W cannot be the origin: the minimum x is 0.9, larger than L₃.

把 W 當成前兩段的目標。由於兩段都長 1，可達區域就是半徑為 2 的圓盤。若 W 在圓盤外，就沒有一組角度能同時符合指定的位置和方向。這個示範中，最小的 x 是 0.9，大於 L₃，所以 W 不會落在原點。

For each two-link solution (θ₁, θ₂), the direction of the second link is θ₁ + θ₂. The third joint turns from that direction to ψ:

對每組雙連桿解 (θ₁, θ₂)，第二段的方向是 θ₁ + θ₂。第三關節要從這個方向轉到 ψ：

θ₃ = ψ − (θ₁ + θ₂) (mod 360°)

03 · REMOVE ONE CONSTRAINT03 · 放開一項限制

What if we only pin the tip?如果只固定末端呢？

If the direction ψ is free, the wrist can lie anywhere on the dashed circle that the first two links can reach. Try the direction slider above without moving the target. As W moves outside the shaded disc, that direction becomes impossible even though the target itself stays in place.

如果不指定方向 ψ，手腕可以位於虛線圓上前兩段連桿能到達的任何一點。試着保持目標不動，只調整上方的方向滑桿。當 W 移到淺藍圓盤外，即使目標仍在原位，機械臂也無法以該方向到達它。

PUT IT TOGETHER整理成一套公式

From tip position and direction to three angles.由末端位置和方向，算出三個角度。

Given the tip (x, y), its direction ψ and the three link lengths, first find W, then plug its coordinates into the two-link equations. Use each resulting pair to find the third joint angle.

已知末端 (x, y)、方向 ψ 和三段連桿長度，先求 W，再把手腕座標代入雙連桿公式。每組 θ₁、θ₂ 都可用來算出第三個角度。

W= (x − L₃ cos ψ, y − L₃ sin ψ)
θ₃≡ ψ − θ₁ − θ₂ (mod 360°)
