WHAT YOU ARE SEEING

One target can have two answers.

01

The elbow lies on two circles

It must be L₁ from the base and L₂ from the target. Their intersections are the only possible elbow positions.

02

Two intersections, two poses

The two intersections sit on opposite sides of OT. Their elbow angles have opposite signs, but both arms reach the same point.

03

No intersection, no solution

If the target is too far away—or too close when the links differ—the arm cannot reach it.
