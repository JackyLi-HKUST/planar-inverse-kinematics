THE MATHEMATICAL IDEA背後的數學

Having a solution at every point does not guarantee a continuous choice.每個點都有解，也不一定能連續地選解。

To follow a solution continuously, a small movement of the target must produce a small change in the pose. At the origin, choosing any one of the folded poses would have to work for every direction of approach.

如果能連續地跟着一組解，目標的一點移動就只會引起姿態的一點改變。在原點選定一個折疊姿態後，無論從哪個方向接近，都必須能接上同一個姿態。

Show the proof展開數學證明

Claim. For two freely rotating links of fixed positive lengths, a continuous choice of pose on the entire reachable set exists if and only if the lengths are unequal. A pose uses angles modulo a full turn. If F maps poses to tip positions, a choice S must satisfy F(S(T)) = T for every reachable target T.

命題。 對兩段長度固定、皆可自由旋轉的連桿，能在整個可達區域連續選擇姿態，當且僅當兩段長度不等。姿態的角度以一整圈為週期。若 F 把姿態映至末端位置，選解 S 必須對每個可達目標 T 滿足 F(S(T)) = T。

1. Equal lengths: two directions give incompatible limits1. 等長：兩個方向給出不相容的極限

At the origin the links point in opposite directions, so every elbow on the circle of radius L gives a folded pose. Can any of those poses be the limit for every approach?

目標在原點時，兩段連桿方向相反，因此半徑為 L 的圓上每個肘點都對應一個折疊姿態。這些姿態之中，有沒有一個可以成為所有接近路徑的極限？

Write the common length as L. For a target T = r u, where u is a unit vector and r > 0, any possible elbow E obeys |E| = |E − r u| = L. Squaring and subtracting gives

令共同長度為 L。對目標 T = r u，其中 u 是單位向量且 r > 0，任何合法肘點 E 都滿足 |E| = |E − r u| = L。把兩式平方後相減，得到

0=r2−2rE·u⇒E·u=r2

If the chosen pose were continuous at O, its elbow would also be continuous there. Taking r → 0 along the positive x-axis would force the chosen elbow at O to have zero x-coordinate. Approaching along the positive y-axis would force its y-coordinate to be zero. That would put the elbow at O, contradicting |E| = L > 0. The argument applies to any choice of poses, including one that tries to switch branches.

若所選姿態在 O 連續，它的肘點也必須在該處連續。沿正 x 軸令 r → 0，會迫使原點所選肘點的 x 座標為零；沿正 y 軸接近，則迫使其 y 座標為零。這樣肘點就要位於 O，與 |E| = L > 0 矛盾。即使嘗試切換分支，這個論證仍適用於任何選解方式。

2. Unequal lengths: construct a continuous choice2. 不等長：構造連續的選解

Now every reachable T satisfies r = |T| ≥ |L₁ − L₂| > 0. Put u = T/r and let Ju be u rotated counterclockwise by 90°. The two distance constraints determine the projection a of the elbow onto u and give its two possible positions:

現在每個可達的 T 都滿足 r = |T| ≥ |L₁ − L₂| > 0。令 u = T/r，並以 Ju 表示把 u 逆時針轉 90° 後的向量。兩個距離條件決定肘點在 u 上的投影 a，給出以下兩個可能位置：

a=r2+L12−L222r E±=au±L12−a2Ju

Choose either sign and keep it fixed. Every term is continuous on the closed reachable annulus: the denominator never vanishes, and the square root is continuous even where it becomes zero. The directions E/L₁ and (T − E)/L₂ determine the pose continuously. This gives a continuous choice all the way to both boundary circles, where the two branches coincide. ∎

選定一個正負號後保持不變。式中各項在整個閉可達圓環上都連續：分母從不為零，而平方根在等於零的地方仍然連續。兩段連桿的方向 E/L₁ 和 (T − E)/L₂ 連續地決定姿態。因此選解可一直延伸到兩條邊界圓；在每條邊界上，兩個分支都會重合。∎
