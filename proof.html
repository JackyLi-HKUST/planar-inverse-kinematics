WHY IT WORKS背後的數學

Why does moving T matter?為甚麼移動 T 會有影響？

With the tip T pinned, the third link forces the wrist W to move on a circle. The first two links still solve the same two-link problem, now with W as their target. The key is whether that wrist circle reaches the edge of the shaded disc.

末端 T 固定後，第三段連桿迫使手腕 W 沿圓周移動。前兩段仍然解的是同一個雙連桿問題，只是目標換成了 W。關鍵在於手腕圓是否碰到淺藍圓盤的邊界。

Show the proof: why one loop or two?展開證明：為何是一個圈或兩個圈？

1. Fix the tip and describe every wrist position1. 固定末端，描述所有手腕位置

Set O = (0,0), T = (d,0) and take link lengths 1, 1, and c = 0.75. Assume 0.9 ≤ d ≤ 2.6, free joints, and no collision constraints. If ψ is the direction of the last link from wrist to tip, then

令 O = (0,0)、T = (d,0)，三段長度為 1、1 和 c = 0.75。假設 0.9 ≤ d ≤ 2.6、關節可自由旋轉，且不考慮碰撞。若 ψ 是第三段由手腕指向末端的方向，則

W(ψ)=(d−ccosψ,−csinψ) |W|2=d2+c2−2dccosψ

In this range W never reaches O, because d > c. For 0 < |W| < 2 there are exactly two poses of the first two links; for |W| = 2 there is one straight pose; and for |W| > 2 there is none. A feasible wrist position and an elbow choice determine the entire arm, since the last link must join W to T.

在此範圍內 d > c，所以 W 不會到達 O。當 0 < |W| < 2，前兩段恰有兩種姿態；當 |W| = 2，只有一種伸直姿態；當 |W| > 2 則無解。選定合法的手腕與肘點後，整支手臂便確定，因為第三段必須連接 W 和 T。

2. Follow the two branches and see where they meet2. 沿兩個分支走，看它們在哪裏相接

  • d < 1.25: even the farthest wrist point has |W| = d + c < 2. Each elbow branch follows a full circle of wrist angles, and their poses never coincide: two separate loops.d < 1.25：即使最遠的手腕點也滿足 |W| = d + c < 2。兩個肘部分支都各自沿完整一圈移動，姿態從不重合，所以是兩個分離的圈。
  • d = 1.25: the full wrist circle is still reachable, but it touches the reach boundary at ψ = π. The two pose loops share that one straight pose, forming a figure eight.d = 1.25：整個手腕圓仍然可達，但在 ψ = π 與可達邊界相切。兩個姿態圈只共用這一個伸直姿態，形成八字形。
  • d > 1.25: only one closed wrist arc is reachable. Indeed, |W| ≤ 2 gives cos ψ ≥ (d² + c² − 4)/(2dc), with the right-hand side strictly between −1 and 1. Each elbow branch gives a copy of that arc; its two endpoints are straight poses shared by both copies. Following one arc out and the other back makes one loop.d > 1.25：只有一段閉圓弧可達。由 |W| ≤ 2 得 cos ψ ≥ (d² + c² − 4)/(2dc)，右邊嚴格介於 −1 和 1 之間。每個分支各有一份這段圓弧；兩個端點都是兩份共用的伸直姿態。沿一個分支走出去，再沿另一個走回來，便形成一個圈。

In the interior of each branch the elbow varies continuously with the wrist, by the two-circle construction. At a straight pose those two elbow positions merge. These are the only identifications. The resulting glued space maps continuously and bijectively onto the space of arm poses; since the former is compact and the latter is Hausdorff, the map is a homeomorphism. So the loops describe the whole space of poses. ∎

由雙圓作圖可知，在每個分支的內部，肘點隨手腕連續移動；到達伸直姿態時，兩個肘點重合。除此之外沒有其他重合。把共用的點接起來後，所得空間到姿態空間的映射是連續雙射；前者緊緻、後者是 Hausdorff 空間，所以這是同胚。這些圈描述的就是整個姿態空間。∎
